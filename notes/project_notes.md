# Project Notes — KPMG Data Analysis Using MS Excel

## Project objective
Analyze customer demographics, transactions, and prospective customers to understand customer behavior and business performance using Microsoft Excel.

## Tasks covered by the course brief
1. **Data Cleaning:** remove duplicates, standardize state names and dates, and correct inconsistent or missing values.
2. **Customer Segmentation:** summarize customers by wealth segment, gender, and job industry.
3. **Transaction Analysis:** review monthly sales, brand and product-line performance, and top customers.
4. **New Customer Insights:** review new-customer demographics, location, property valuation, and potential value.
5. **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):** calculate CLV and compare it across customer segments.
6. **Executive Summary and Recommendations:** summarize findings and recommend marketing, geographic, and product improvements.

## Findings recorded in the submitted executive summary
The uploaded Task 6 PDF reports the following findings:
- The largest customer wealth segment is Mass Customer.
- Male customers make more bike-related purchases than female customers.
- Financial, Health, Manufacturing, and Unknown are reported as industries with high customer counts.
- The reported high-sales months are October, May, July, August, and January.
- Giant Bicycle is reported as the top-selling brand, and Standard as the top product line.
- The top 10 customers are reported to generate approximately 15%–20% of revenue.
- New South Wales is reported as the state with the highest number of new customers.
- Manufacturing and Financial Services are reported as the highest-CLV industries.

These findings are transcribed from the submitted summary and have not been independently recalculated in this notes file.

## Recommendations recorded in the submitted executive summary
- Run targeted marketing campaigns for high-CLV customers.
- Focus expansion on states or regions where new-customer demand is high.
- Refine product lines and brand strategy based on transaction performance and customer behavior.
